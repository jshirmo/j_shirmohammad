Work / Research

Research rooted in curiosity.

Jasmine has been involved in research since her first year at Tulane and works in the Tasker Lab, where research examines how electrophysiological, synaptic, and hormonal signals shape neuroendocrine systems, stress responses, and behavior.

Research environment

Stress and synaptic plasticity

The Tasker Lab studies stress-related neural circuits and synaptic plasticity, with a focus on the hypothalamus and amygdala—brain regions central to stress, homeostasis, and emotional behavior.

Approach

Build from questions

Through this research experience, Jasmine is developing the habit of asking precise questions, examining evidence carefully, and communicating what matters clearly.
