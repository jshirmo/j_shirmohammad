Work / Volunteer work

Service grounded in consistency.

Jasmine’s volunteer work with Special Olympics and older-adult communities reflects her commitment to advocacy, inclusion, and meaningful connection.

Community

Memory care

Stories create connection

At Poydras Home’s Memory Care Unit in New Orleans, Jasmine connects with residents by inviting them to share their stories and life experiences. At the end of the semester, she created a small children’s book about one resident’s life.
