Work / Spanish

Language as a bridge to care.

As a neuroscience student minoring in Spanish, Jasmine pairs language and cultural study with preparation for patient-centered healthcare communication.

Medical Spanish

Communicate in clinical settings

In Spanish for the medical profession, Jasmine practices using Spanish in clinical contexts, including Spanish-language objective structured clinical examinations (OSCEs). These structured encounters strengthen her ability to listen, explain, and respond clearly in patient conversations.

Perspective

Attend to culture and context

Her Spanish minor pairs language study with coursework in Hispanic and Latin American cultures, reinforcing that healthcare experiences are shaped by cultural context, values, and lived experience. Jasmine hopes to build stronger relationships with future patients by meeting them with greater understanding in the language they prefer.
